Important — read before generating

Please check these points before joining the queue.

  • Choose the correct service. Read the service guidance above; if unsure, ask the office before generating a token.
  • Prepare your documents. Keep your student ID, receipts, forms, and supporting documents relevant to your request ready.
  • Wait times are estimates only. Your turn may be earlier or later depending on request complexity and staff availability.
  • Watch for your token. Keep the queue page open and pay attention when your token is called. If you miss your turn, staff may skip it.
  • If skipped, ask the service desk. Staff can advise you and may requeue your token at the end of the line.
  • Keep your details private. Save your token number and register number; you need both to check your personal status.

ⓘ Your token joins the selected service queue. Please follow any additional instructions shown by the college office.
